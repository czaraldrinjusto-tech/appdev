# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things
 
## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror series created by *The Duffer Brothers*.  
It’s set in the 1980s in a small town called Hawkins, Indiana, where a group of kids encounters mysterious events caused by secret government experiments and a supernatural world known as the **Upside Down**.
 
 
## Main Characters
1. **Eleven** — A girl with psychokinetic abilities.  
2. **Mike Wheeler** — The brave leader of the group.  
3. **Dustin Henderson** — Known for his intelligence and humor.  
4. **Lucas Sinclair** — Logical and cautious.  
5. **Jim Hopper** — The protective town sheriff.
 
 
## Favorite Quote
> “Friends don’t lie.” — Eleven
 
 
## Why I Love It
- 1980s *nostalgia*  
- Strong **friendship** and teamwork  
- Suspenseful and emotional storytelling  
- Great soundtrack and cinematography  
 
 
## Summary
*Stranger Things* mixes science fiction, horror, and adventure perfectly.  
It shows how friendship and courage can overcome fear — even against monsters from another world.
